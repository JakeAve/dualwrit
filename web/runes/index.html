ᚦᛖ ᚱᚢᚾᛖᛋ ᚩᚠ ᚦᛖ ᚻᚩᛒᛒᛁᛏ The Runes of The Hobbit

J.R.R. Tolkien used Anglo-Saxon runes to write modern English in The Hobbit, on Thror's map and on the title page. Here each rune stands in for a letter, so a word is written the way it is spelled, not the way it sounds.

The runes

Each rune stands for the letter marked in its example word. Under each word is how it is written in runes. Press a speaker to hear the word.

The letters

Three letters have no rune of their own: ᛁ also writes j, ᚢ also writes v, and q is written as cw.

Two letters, one rune

Some pairs of letters get one rune of their own, wherever the two fall together in a word.

Try reading

Read each one letter by letter, then press it to check.
